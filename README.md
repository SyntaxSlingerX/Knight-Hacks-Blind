# AriaVision

**An AI-powered assistant that uses Meta Aria glasses to help users understand their surroundings through spoken descriptions and answers.**

## About

AriaVision explores how artificial intelligence and wearable technology can make understanding the world around us more accessible, especially for people who are blind or visually impaired.

By connecting Meta Aria glasses to an AI assistant, AriaVision helps users interpret visual information from their surroundings, ask questions, and receive spoken responses without constantly needing to look at a screen.

## Features

- **Environmental Understanding:** Uses visual information captured by Meta Aria glasses to help users understand their surroundings.
- **AI-Powered Questions:** Allows users to ask questions about their environment, such as "What is in front of me?"
- **Spoken Responses:** Provides audio responses so users can listen to information instead of reading it.
- **Wearable Technology Integration:** Connects Meta Aria glasses to a computer to support the assistant's functionality.
- **Accessibility-Focused Design:** Explores a more hands-free way to access information about the surrounding environment.

## How It Works

1. Meta Aria glasses capture visual information from the user's surroundings.
2. A Python bridge transfers information between the glasses and the application.
3. Gemini helps interpret visual information and generate relevant responses.
4. Users can ask questions through the application.
5. The assistant responds through audio.

## Inspiration

We wanted to explore how AI could make everyday experiences more accessible for people who are blind or visually impaired. By combining wearable cameras with AI-powered assistance, we created AriaVision to help users better understand their surroundings through audio.

## Challenges

During development, we worked through challenges involving Python dependencies, connecting the glasses to the computer, USB permissions, network restrictions, and communication between the frontend and Python bridge. These experiences taught us how complex hardware and software integration can be.

## What's Next

- Gather feedback from potential users to guide future improvements.

## Safety Note

AriaVision is an experimental project. AI-generated descriptions may be inaccurate or incomplete, so the application should not replace established mobility aids or independent safety checks.
